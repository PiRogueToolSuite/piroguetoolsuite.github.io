---
title: "Browse to dashboard"
---

You should access dashboard browsing to `https://pirogue.local/dashboard` if your default installation has `SYSTEM_HOSTNAME: pirogue`.

{{< callout context="note" title="Details" icon="info-circle" >}}
Depending on PiRogue variables, `EXTERNAL_ADDRESS` and `SYSTEM_HOSTNAME` is where the dashboard is exposed.

It's independently accessible at **https://`EXTERNAL_ADDRESS`/dashboard** or **https://`SYSTEM_HOSTNAME`.local/dashboard**.

See [Administration concepts](/docs/pirogue/version_2.x/configuration/#administration-concepts) for more details.
{{< /callout >}}
